Get started
Up and running in 3 steps
1

Download & double-click

Download the installer above. Double-click it — Terminal opens for ~30 seconds and sets everything up automatically. Then you can close Terminal.

2

Browser opens automatically

Your Mac control panel opens in the browser with a pairing code. The agent runs silently in the background from now on.

3

Pair your iPhone

The Mac app shows you a mobile link. Open it on your iPhone, enter the code — you're connected and ready to launch any app.

Already installed?

Enter your Room ID (shown in the Mac app) to open the apps directly.
